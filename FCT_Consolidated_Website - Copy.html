The Origin Story

Why Started Fridrick Cyber Tech

Fridrick Charles

Founder & Trust Intelligence Advisor

Connect on LinkedIn

Over the years, our founder noticed the same pattern repeating across industries.

Organizations invested in security tools. Teams worked hard to achieve compliance. Audit reports were completed. Yet when enterprise buyers, investors, regulators, or board members asked difficult questions, confidence was still missing.

The problem was rarely a lack of controls. The problem was a lack of clarity.

Leaders were being asked to make important business decisions using technical reports, compliance checklists, and fragmented information that did not translate into clear action.

We founded Fridrick Cyber Tech to bridge that gap.

Our mission is simple: help organizations understand cyber risk in business terms, make better decisions, and build trust with the people who matter most.

“Organizations are not losing deals because they lack certifications. They lose deals because they are not prepared for enterprise trust reviews.”

Whether that pressure comes from a customer security review, an investor due diligence process, a board meeting, or a regulatory requirement, our role is to help leaders move forward with confidence.

– FRIDRICK CYBER TECH

Advisory FAQ

Questions about the advisory process.

How long does a Revenue Trust Sprint take?

Typically 2–3 weeks, depending on the size of the questionnaire, evidence availability and stakeholder responsiveness.

Do you guarantee buyer approval?

No. The buyer controls their own assessment. FCT helps you prepare and communicate your current position more clearly.

Do we need to be certified already?

Not necessarily. The Sprint helps you understand the requirements and current readiness. Any certification pathway is separate.

Is this a penetration test?

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Is this a compliance certification or audit?

No. Board Scrutiny Readiness is an advisory proposition. It helps leadership understand risk, business impact and decision priorities. Any formal audit, certification or attestation requires a separately scoped engagement with the appropriate qualified parties.

Does FCT replace our CISO, GRC platform or security tools?

No. FCT is positioned as a decision and advisory layer that can work alongside internal security teams, existing platforms and specialist providers. The scope depends on the organisation's needs.

How does Cyber Risk Quantification help leadership?

CRQ connects cyber-risk scenarios to potential financial consequences using explicit assumptions, ranges and uncertainty. It supports prioritisation and investment decisions; it does not predict losses with certainty.

Which frameworks and regulations can be considered?

Depending on the organisation and engagement scope, relevant references may include SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR, India's DPDP Act, HIPAA and PCI DSS. Applicability must be confirmed for each organisation and jurisdiction.

What happens after the readiness assessment?

Your answers provide an indicative baseline. If the business trigger warrants it, FCT can discuss the context, validate assumptions and propose a monthly advisory scope combining security leadership and business-risk decision support. The assessment itself does not automatically create a client engagement.
